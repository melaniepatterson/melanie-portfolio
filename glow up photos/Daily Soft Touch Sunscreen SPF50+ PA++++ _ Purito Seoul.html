  • FAQ

    Toggle accordion

    • What do SPF and PA mean?

      SPF stands for "sun protection factor," which measures a sunscreen's ability to protect against UVB rays, while PA is a rating indicating protection against UVA rays. The Daily Soft Touch Sunscreen provides broad-spectrum protection with SPF 50+ and PA++++.

    • Is this product reef-safe?

      Yes, the Daily Soft Touch Sunscreen is free of ingredients harmful to coral reefs and marine life.

    • Can I use this sunscreen on my kids?

      While this sunscreen has been gently formulated and clinically tested for sensitive skin, we still recommend consulting with a pediatrician and conducting a patch test to ensure compatibility.
